# Code of Conduct

Orbit Desktop is a small project built around a calm, respectful desktop experience. The same standard applies to its community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Welcome different distributions, compositors, accessibility needs, and levels of experience.
- Keep reports reproducible and remove private data from logs and screenshots.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and knowingly sharing credentials or private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact options. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project constructive.
